Name & mark

"Copy that" is how you tell someone they were heard.

Radio operators say it to confirm a message arrived. The mark is a loud wave that decays into a tick. The complaint gets quieter, then it gets closed. The same tick ends every status in the product.

    REPORT

    Faster than typing a paragraph

    Pick a sound, hold to measure, send. Location, time and the reading travel with it, so an officer knows what to look for.

    TRACK

    Five stages, plain words

    Heard, matched, dispatched, on scene, closed. Every change sends one text. Silence is never the status.

    PATTERN

    Your report counts toward something

    Reports from the same block and address are grouped. Repeat addresses get escalated so one neighbor does not carry the whole case.

    CLOSURE

    The last word is yours

    The outcome says what the city did. You answer "quiet now" or "still loud". Still loud reopens the case with the first one on file.
