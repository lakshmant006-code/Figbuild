02 · The city has ears

Is it just you, or the whole block?

Complaints show up as clusters, so you can tell a one-off from a neighborhood-wide problem. Hotter hexagons mean more reports in the last 30 days. Tap a neighborhood name to fly in, City to zoom back out, Skyline for the view from the harbor and Street level to stand on the avenue. Buildings come from the NYC 3D Building Model. Every tower over 150 ft is on the map, and the orange ring marks a repeat location. Press Diorama for a toy-like isometric slice of the neighborhood you picked. Press Night for the after-dark view: orange pulses are complaints coming in, soft blue pulses are agencies responding.

Concept for the NYC noise complaint design challenge. The 610,000 yearly figure comes from the brief (NYC Open Data, 311).
